Copy in an Obsidian vault

A one-time copy, not a sync: the vault on this computer is only read, never changed, and edits made in Serendipity stay in Serendipity. Everything goes to Serendipity › Second brain, in the vault's own folders. [[Links]] become note links, Excalidraw drawings and canvases come in as sharp pictures, and pictures and files go to Attachments once each. Running it again later only adds what isn't there yet, so it's fine to stop and carry on later.
