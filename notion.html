Copy notes in from Notion

A one-time copy, not a sync: your Notion pages stay as they are, and edits made in Quick Notes stay in Quick Notes. Pages go to Readables › Notes › Notion, with sub-pages in folders named after their parent page, and images and files in Notion › Attachments. Running it again later only adds pages that aren't there yet.

Demo mode: this copy uses a pretend Drive. Use the real app at vikmmn.github.io/quick-notes/notion.html to copy for real.

1. Export from Notion

  1. In Notion: Settings › General (or Workspace › Settings) › Export all workspace content. For one page and its sub-pages: the page's ••• menu › Export.
  2. Export format Markdown & CSV, include content Everything, and turn on Include subpages and Create folders for subpages.
  3. Notion emails a download link. Save the zip to this device, or to your Google Drive.

2. Choose the export

Sign in with Google to read the export from your Drive and save the notes.

Or choose the export zip from this device:

Your Google sign-in ran out. The copy is paused, nothing is lost.

The screen stays on while this runs. If you switch away, it carries on when you come back. It's fine to stop and run it again: pages already copied are skipped.
