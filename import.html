Copy notes in from Google Keep

A one-time copy, not a sync: Google Keep stays as it is, and edits made in Quick Notes stay in Quick Notes. Brings over every note from a Google Takeout export of Keep: notes and checklists, pinned, archived and trashed notes, labels, colours, dates, saved links, and images, drawings and voice recordings. They go to Quick Notes › Keep. Running it again later only adds what isn't there yet.

Demo mode: this copy uses a pretend Drive. Use the real app at vikmmn.github.io/quick-notes/import.html to import for real.
