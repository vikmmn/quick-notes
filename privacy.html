Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Your notes stay in your own Google Drive

Serendip saves each note as a Markdown file in a folder in your own Google Drive, and keeps a copy on your device so it works offline. Serendip has no server that stores, reads or analyses your notes.

What Serendip can reach in your Drive

Serendip asks Google only for access to the files and folders it creates, plus any file you choose yourself in Google's file picker (for example a Google Keep or Notion export you want to copy in). It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

Signing in

So you stay signed in, a small sign-in service turns Google's one-time sign-in code into a lasting permission. That permission is sealed with a key only the service has and is kept on your device, not on the service. The service never sees your notes and keeps no records of you. You can withdraw the permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. Serendip' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Your notes are files in your Drive: delete the Serendip folder to delete them. Clearing the app's site data in your browser removes the copy on your device.
